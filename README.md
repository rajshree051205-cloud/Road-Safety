# 🌙 NightWatch

**AI-powered personal safety for night travel.**
Real-time route scoring, crowd intelligence, and emergency response, built to help people get home safely after dark.

> 🚧 **Status:** In active development. Features and details below may change.

---

## Overview

Travelling at night can be stressful, especially in unfamiliar areas or on poorly lit roads. NightWatch helps you choose safer routes, stay connected to people you trust, and get help fast if something goes wrong.

The app combines live safety scoring, community-reported incidents, trip tracking, and a one-tap (or voice-activated) SOS system into a single experience.

---

## Features

### 🛡️ AI Safety Score
- Live safety score (0-100) for your current location, refreshed every 30 seconds
- Score factors include:
  - 💡 Lighting
  - 👥 Crowd levels
  - 🚨 Recent incidents (last 48 hours)
  - 🚓 Distance to the nearest police presence
- Clear labels and a short plain-language explanation for each score

### 🧭 Route Intelligence
- Compare multiple routes to the same destination
- Each route shows a safety percentage, travel time, lighting, crowd activity, and number of safety flags
- Safest route is highlighted as the recommendation, so you can weigh a few extra minutes against a safer path

### 🗺️ Live Safety Heatmap
- Colour-coded map of high-risk, caution, and safe zones
- Your current position shown alongside nearby zones
- Community incident feed with distance and time since each report

### 🆘 SOS & Emergency Response
- One-tap SOS button
- **Smart voice detection** for trigger phrases (for example "help me", "save me", "call police", and Hindi keywords like "bachaao") plus panic tone and scream detection
- When SOS activates:
  - Live location is shared with your trusted circle
  - Audio recording starts as evidence
  - Silent SMS and push alerts go to trusted contacts
  - Emergency services (112 in India) are contacted with your location
- "I'm Safe" option to cancel an SOS

### 👥 Trusted Circle
- Add family and friends who can watch your trip live
- Automatic alerts when someone in the circle goes off-route
- Configurable circle settings:
  - Route deviation alerts (e.g. 300 m off the planned path)
  - Inactivity detection (no movement for 5 minutes)
  - Safe arrival confirmation
  - Low battery warning
  - Predictive risk alerts for approaching danger zones

### 🔔 Safety Alerts
- Notifications about high-risk zones on your route
- Street light outage and low-light zone warnings
- Circle member updates (deviations, safe arrivals)
- Safety score changes in your area

### 📍 Safe Trip Tracking
- Start a tracked trip along your chosen route
- Circle members can follow progress until you arrive

---

## How It Works

1. **Allow location access** (or enter a destination manually).
2. **Check your area's safety score** to understand your surroundings.
3. **Compare routes** and pick the one that balances safety and time.
4. **Start a trip** so your trusted circle can follow along.
5. **Stay protected:** NightWatch monitors for deviations, inactivity, and risk zones.
6. **Trigger SOS** by tapping the button or saying a keyword if you ever feel unsafe.

---

## Privacy & Safety Notes

- Location is used only to calculate safety scores and to power trip tracking and SOS.
- Trusted contacts are chosen by you and can be managed at any time.
- Audio recording only begins when SOS is activated.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** NightWatch is a safety aid and does not replace official emergency services. In an emergency, always contact local authorities directly (**112** in India).
